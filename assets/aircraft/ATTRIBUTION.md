# Aircraft photo attribution

The bundled photographs are resized copies of real aircraft photographs. They are displayed with a common `BoxFit.cover` crop in the app.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `b737.jpg` | Lufthansa Boeing 737-500 (D-ABIR) at Frankfurt Airport | MaxEddi | [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boeing_B737.jpg), released into the public domain by the author |
| `a350.jpg` | Airbus A350 at Farnborough Airshow 2016 | pjs2005 | [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Airbus_A350_(28227541585).jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/) |

The A320 and A350 files are adapted copies and remain under their respective ShareAlike licenses. They are included locally so aircraft photos work offline.
